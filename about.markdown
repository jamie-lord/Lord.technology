I'm Jamie Lord. I work as a Solution Architect at [CDS](https://www.cds.co.uk/), a digital consultancy that mostly serves the UK public sector.

Most of my time goes on Single Online Home, a national policing platform that serves almost every UK police force in England and Wales. It's a hybrid Azure and Cloudflare estate, which means the tools I reach for at work are often the tools I end up writing about here.

A lot of my attention these days is on agentic engineering — how teams actually work with AI tools like Claude Code day-to-day, rather than treating them as novelties. I wrote the section of [CDS's engineering handbook](https://handbook.cds.co.uk/) that covers our practice: working with `CLAUDE.md` files, using TDD to keep agents honest, reviewing AI-written PRs properly. Alongside that I build MCP servers for enterprise data, and I wrote the AI governance framework we use with public-sector clients.

Separately, I'm writing [*Architecting on Cloudflare*](https://architectingoncloudflare.com) — a book about Cloudflare's Developer Platform for people evaluating edge-native design against AWS, Azure, or GCP. It's in beta now.

Before CDS I co-founded HeadClear in 2021 and spent two years as CTO. We built a privacy-preserving employee wellbeing platform — vital signs measured through a phone camera, no stored video, ML models for anomaly detection. Before that I took Flexipay from a legacy desktop payroll product to a multi-tenant SaaS serving a few thousand UK organisations.

It's still pretty rare that I'm not working on [a side-project or two]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) ([more on GitHub](https://github.com/jamie-lord)). I especially enjoy Blazor for rapid prototyping — something I've been doing since version 0.1.0 back in 2018. Longer-form writing that doesn't fit a technical blog lives at [nearlyright.com](https://nearlyright.com). This site is, by choice, a Jekyll static site I shaped myself.
