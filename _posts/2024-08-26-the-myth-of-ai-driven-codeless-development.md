[AWS CEO Matt Garman told an internal meeting](https://www.businessinsider.com/aws-ceo-developers-stop-coding-ai-takes-over-2024-8) that within two years most developers might stop coding altogether. This is what someone says when they confuse coding with typing.

Coding is the act of forcing a vague requirement into something precise enough to execute. The work happens before any line is written: deciding what 'allow users to share content easily' actually means, which platforms count, what the privacy boundary is, how it degrades on a slow network. A model can produce a function once you have answered those questions. It cannot make you ask them.

The same gap shows up in debugging and in the architectural decisions that determine whether a system survives its second year. AI assistants will absorb more of the typing. The thinking is the job, and the job is not going anywhere in two years.
